Notify me about games

Tell us your team and your city. We will email you when a new game goes on sale, send a weekly round-up with prices and remind you a week before kick-off.

What you get

  • A weekly round-up of upcoming games with that week's prices.
  • A reminder a week before the game you picked.
  • Now and then, a hotel or tour in the game's city, only if you showed interest in it.

We keep your email, name, team, city and language in the email tools we use (Brevo, in France, and MailerLite, in Ireland), only to send these alerts. We never sell your data. See the privacy policy for more. PRIVACY AND CONTACT
