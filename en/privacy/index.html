Privacy policy

Last updated October 16, 2026.

Who we are

MyTickets Tours (mytickets.tours) is an affiliate site for sports and concert tickets, run by Everton Bras.

We do not sell tickets. Purchases happen on the SportsEvents365 site, which is responsible for the sale, payment and delivery.

What data we handle

Browsing: the services that host the site (Microsoft Azure and GitHub) log technical data, such as IP address and browser type, to keep the site running and secure.

Sale alerts: if you ask for an alert, we keep your email, the event you chose and your language, with your consent. The sign-up reaches our inbox through the FormSubmit service.

WhatsApp support: if you message us, we handle your number and messages to reply, through Meta's platform.

Pinterest: event and guide pages have a button to save the page to Pinterest. Pinterest's code only loads after you accept cookies; before that, the button is a plain link to Pinterest, and whatever you save there falls under Pinterest's privacy policy.

Cookies: we use Google Analytics to measure visits and clicks, and it only sets cookies after you click Accept in the cookie notice. If you decline, no analytics or advertising cookies are set. To change your choice, clear this site's data in your browser and the notice will show again.

Affiliate links: when you click a link to SportsEvents365, the partner records that you came from MyTickets (code a_aid) to calculate our commission. From then on, the SportsEvents365 privacy policy applies.

Why we use it

To send the alert you asked for, reply to your messages and keep the site running and secure. We never sell or rent your data.

Legal basis

Consent for alerts, support and analytics (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a); LGPD Art. 7, I). Legitimate interest for security and operation of the site (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f); LGPD Art. 7, IX).

How long we keep it

Alert emails: until the event takes place or until you unsubscribe, whichever comes first. WhatsApp conversations: up to 12 months.

Sharing and international transfers

Your data only goes through the providers named above (Microsoft, GitHub, FormSubmit, Meta, Google and Pinterest). They may process data outside your country under their own contractual safeguards.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or port your data, and withdraw consent at any time. Write to email@mytickets.tours and we will reply within 15 days.

You can also complain to the data protection authority in your country.

Minors

The site is not aimed at people under 18. Ticket purchases must be made by adults.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page will be updated.

Contact

Responsible person: Everton Bras
E-mail: email@mytickets.tours

MYTICKETS.TOURS

Prices are set by sellers and may be above or below the face value printed on the ticket. When you buy through our links we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Club, league and event names are used for identification only; we have no official connection with them.
