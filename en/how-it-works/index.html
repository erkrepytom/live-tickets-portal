How it works

MyTickets Tours is an event guide with affiliate links. Sale, payment and delivery are handled by Ticketmaster. Here is what that means for you.

Who does what

MyTickets writes the guides (date, venue, getting there, sections) and points to the right partner page. We do not sell tickets and never take your payment.

Ticketmaster, part of Live Nation Entertainment, is a ticketing platform. It processes the order, charges you and delivers the tickets under its own purchase policy.

When you buy through our link, we may earn a commission. Your price is the same.

Standard or resale tickets

A standard ticket is sold for the first time on behalf of the event organizer. A resale ticket is listed by fans or professional resellers.

On Ticketmaster's seat map each ticket is marked as standard or resale. Resale tickets follow demand and can cost more than face value.

Many events have a ticket limit per person, shown under 'More Info' on the event page.

Delivery

For most events the ticket is digital and sits in the Ticketmaster app or account.

When ticket transfer is not available, everyone on the order enters together, and screenshots or PDFs are not accepted.

Cancellations and postponements

If the event is cancelled, Ticketmaster refunds the original payment method with no action needed from you.

If the event is postponed or rescheduled, your ticket stays valid. Refunds in that case depend on the organizer.

Otherwise all sales are final. Check the event, date and seats before you pay.

How we keep information current

We check each event's date and time and show on the page when it was last checked.

Past events are removed on the next update.

Spotted a mistake? Write to email@mytickets.tours and we will fix it.
