Getting there

  • Rod Laver Arena is in Melbourne Park, about a 15-minute walk from Flinders Street station.
  • Extra trams run to Melbourne Park during the tournament. Check times on the Australian Open site.

Seating tips

  • Check the session on the ticket: the men's final has its own session.
  • Seats along the sides show both players.
  • Bring sunscreen: January is summer in Melbourne.

What to bring and what gets refused

  • Check the session printed on the ticket: the final has its own session, separate from the day's other matches.
  • Bags are checked at the complex entrance. Large bags may have to go to the cloakroom.
  • Bring a photo ID, sunscreen and an empty water bottle if the venue allows refills.

Best section for each fan

  • Families. Side seats in middle rows: you see both players without turning your head every point.
  • Budget. Upper levels cost far less and, in a covered arena, the view stays good.

Before you buy

In Victoria, the law caps resale of major-event tickets at 10% above face value unless the seller is authorised. Compare with the official price on ausopen.com before you buy.

Source: Governo de Victoria
