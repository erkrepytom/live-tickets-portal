The men's final of the year's first Grand Slam, on the main court at Melbourne Park.

Getting there

  • Rod Laver Arena is in Melbourne Park, about a 15-minute walk from Flinders Street station.
  • Extra trams run to Melbourne Park during the tournament. Check times on the Australian Open site.

Seating tips

  • Check the session on the ticket: the men's final has its own session.
  • Seats along the sides show both players.
  • Bring sunscreen: January is summer in Melbourne.
