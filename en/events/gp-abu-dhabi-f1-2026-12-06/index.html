The final round of the Formula 1 season, a race that starts at dusk and finishes under the Yas Marina floodlights.

Getting there

  • The circuit is on Yas Island, about 30 minutes by car from central Abu Dhabi.
  • Organizers usually run shuttle buses on race weekend. Check the official GP site.

Seating tips

  • Grandstands near slow corners show more overtakes.
  • Check whether the ticket covers one day or the whole weekend.
  • Bring ear protection.
