Getting there

  • RER A, metro line 1 or tram T2 to La Défense–Grande Arche.
  • The arena is about a 10-minute walk from the station.

Seating tips

  • Floor is closest to the stage; seats show the whole show.
  • Seats facing the stage have the best sound.
  • For a group, buy all seats in one order.

What to bring and what gets refused

  • Arenas usually limit bag sizes and ban professional cameras.
  • Bring a photo ID and the ticket in whatever format the partner sends.
  • Doors open well before the show. Arriving early means a shorter queue for the floor.

Best section for each fan

  • Families. Reserved seats facing the stage: a guaranteed spot and the best sound.
  • Up front. Standing floor: closest to the artist, but you need to arrive early and stand all night.

Before you buy

Celine Dion's Paris shows have drawn many fake-ticket scams. Do not pay a private person by transfer, and do not buy from a screenshot or photo of a ticket.
