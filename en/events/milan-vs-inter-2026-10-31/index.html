Getting there

  • Metro M5 to San Siro Stadio station, next to the stadium.
  • Tram 16 also stops at the square in front of the stadium.

Seating tips

  • Side sections give the best view of the whole pitch.
  • Upper tiers are usually the cheapest.
  • For a group, buy all seats in one order so you sit together.

What to bring and what gets refused

  • Bring your passport or a photo ID. Resale tickets sometimes carry another person's name, and the partner explains by email what to show.
  • Backpacks and large bags are often refused at European stadiums. Carry only the essentials in a small bag.
  • Bottles, flares and sharp objects are banned. Security checks can be slow at big matches.

Best section for each fan

  • Families. Side stands and middle tiers: good view, calmer atmosphere and easier exit.
  • Loud support. Behind the goals is where the ultras stand. It is the loudest area, and almost everyone stands.
  • Your team's end. The partner separates home and away sections. Pick your team's side and avoid wearing the rival shirt in the wrong section.
  • At San Siro. The Curva Sud is home to Milan's ultras and the Curva Nord to Inter's. Both are packed for the derby. For a calmer view, choose the side stands.
